# Privacy Policy — Orbit Mall: Station Tycoon

**Last Updated: November 24, 2026**

## 1. Information We Collect

Orbit Mall: Station Tycoon does not collect, sell, or share users' personal information, and we (the developer) do not run any servers that receive data from the game.

The game does not require account registration and does not include advertising or analytics tracking.

## 2. Game Progress

Game progress, settings, and preferences are saved on the player's device so players can save and continue their games.

**iCloud save sync.** If the player is signed in to iCloud on their device, the game also keeps a copy of their save slots in their own iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This lets the player continue on a new or reinstalled iPhone. This copy is stored and handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we cannot see or access it. Players who do not want this can sign out of iCloud on their device; the game then saves on the device only.

Saved game information is never transmitted to our servers.

## 3. Game Center

The game supports Apple Game Center for achievements and leaderboards. If the player is signed in to Game Center, their achievements and scores (such as best year and station level) are sent to Apple's Game Center service and may be visible to other players according to the player's Game Center settings. Game Center is provided and operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive the player's Game Center account details. Players can sign out of Game Center in their device's settings.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Apart from the Apple services described above (iCloud and Game Center), Orbit Mall does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics services, or tracking tools.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data stored on the device remains until it is deleted. Players can delete a save slot from within the game, or remove all locally stored game data by deleting the application. Save copies in iCloud are deleted when the player deletes the save slot in the game, and can also be cleared through Apple's iCloud account management. Device backups may be managed separately by the operating system.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Orbit Mall does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change.
