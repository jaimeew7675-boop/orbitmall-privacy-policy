# Privacy Policy — Orbit Mall: Station Tycoon

**Last Updated: November 21, 2026**

## 1. Information We Collect

Orbit Mall: Station Tycoon does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share users' personal information.

The game does not require account registration and does not include advertising or analytics tracking.

## 2. Game Progress

Game progress, settings, and preferences are saved locally on the player's device.

This information is used solely to allow players to save and continue their games.

Saved game information is not transmitted to our servers.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Orbit Mall does not integrate third-party advertising networks, analytics services, or tracking tools.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data remains stored locally on the device until deleted. Players can remove locally stored game data by deleting the application. Device backups may be managed separately by the operating system.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Orbit Mall does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change.
