# Orbit Mall: Station Tycoon — Support

Welcome to the official support page for Orbit Mall: Station Tycoon!

## Need Help?

If you're experiencing problems with the game, have questions, or want to report a bug, we're here to help.

**Contact Email:** support@henryhop.com

## Common Questions

**How do I save my game?**

Open the game's settings menu to access the save feature.

**Can I play offline?**

Yes! Orbit Mall is designed to work without an internet connection.

**Does the game require an account?**

No. You can play without creating an account.

**Where is my saved progress?**

Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

## Report a Bug

When reporting a problem, please include:

- Your iPhone model
- Your iOS version
- A description of the problem
- A screenshot, if possible
